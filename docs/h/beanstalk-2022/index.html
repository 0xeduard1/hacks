Beanstalk

🗳️ Governance attack
💥 $181M$181M drained, about $76M kept by the attacker Apr 17, 2022dateEthereumchain🟡 mediumto understand

Voting power was counted at the moment of the vote. The attacker borrowed a huge amount with a flash loan, voted his own proposal through, and paid the loan back.

  1. 🏰

    What was it?

    Beanstalk was a stablecoin protocol governed by its token holders, with an emergency way to execute proposals quickly.

  2. 💥

    What happened

    The attacker flash-borrowed enough tokens for a supermajority, passed a proposal that sent the treasury to himself, executed it, and repaid the loan, all in one transaction.

  3. 🗳️

    The bug, in plain words

    Votes were measured "right now", and a passed proposal could execute immediately, with no delay.

  4. 🛡️

    How it could have been stopped

    Measure voting power at an earlier snapshot and add a timelock between approval and execution.

  5. 🧙

    The lesson for an auditor

    Anything measured in the current block can be borrowed for one block.

Meet the monster: 🗳️ The Vote Buyer

Voting power is measured at the moment of the vote, so an attacker borrows tokens with a flash loan, votes, executes, and returns the loan in one transaction.
