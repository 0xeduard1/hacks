Mango Markets

🎭 Oracle manipulation
💥 $115Mabout $115M of bad debt Oct 11, 2022dateSolanachain🟡 mediumto understand

The attacker pumped the price of a thinly traded token, used the inflated position as collateral, and borrowed everything else in the protocol.

  1. 🏰

    What was it?

    Mango Markets was a trading and lending protocol on Solana that let users borrow against their positions.

  2. 💥

    What happened

    Using two accounts, the attacker pushed up the price of MNGO, Mango's own thin token, then borrowed real assets against the now "valuable" position.

  3. 🎭

    The bug, in plain words

    The protocol trusted a price that a single well-funded trader could move, and let that price back big loans.

  4. 🛡️

    How it could have been stopped

    Robust, manipulation-resistant oracles and strict limits on collateral from illiquid tokens.

  5. 🧙

    The lesson for an auditor

    A price is only as safe as the market behind it. The person behind it was later arrested; "it's just a trading strategy" did not hold up.

Meet the monster: 🎭 The Price Faker

A protocol trusts a price that can be pushed around, usually a thinly traded token. The attacker pumps it, uses it as collateral, and borrows real assets.
