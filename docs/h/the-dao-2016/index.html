The DAO

🌀 Reentrancy
💥 $60Mabout 3.6M ETH, roughly $60M at the time Jun 17, 2016dateEthereumchain🟢 easyto understand

The first famous smart contract hack: a withdraw function sent ETH before updating the balance, so the attacker withdrew again and again.

  1. 🏰

    What was it?

    The DAO was a community investment fund that lived entirely in a smart contract. People sent ETH in and got tokens that let them vote on projects.

  2. 💥

    What happened

    An attacker used a contract that, every time it received ETH, called the withdraw function again. The DAO kept paying because the balance was only updated at the end.

  3. 🌀

    The bug, in plain words

    Wrong order of steps: send money first, update the books later. That gap is where the attacker kept re-entering.

  4. 🛡️

    How it could have been stopped

    Update the balance before sending money (checks-effects-interactions) and add a reentrancy guard.

  5. 🧙

    The lesson for an auditor

    Whenever a contract talks to the outside world, assume the outside world can call back in. Ethereum later hard-forked to return the funds, which created Ethereum Classic.

Meet the monster: 🌀 The Echo

A contract sends money (or calls another contract) before it updates its own records. The receiver calls back in, again and again, and each time the contract still thinks the money is there.
