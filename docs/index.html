Curve pools (Vyper bug)

Jul 30, 2023 on Ethereum 💥 $69M

The pools had a reentrancy lock, but some versions of the Vyper compiler built it wrong. The lock silently did nothing.

🛠️ Compiler / tooling bug 🧮

Euler Finance

Mar 13, 2023 on Ethereum 💥 $197M

A little-used donate function let users reduce their own collateral without checking if the position was still healthy.

🧮 Broken invariantFunds returned 🎭

Mango Markets

Oct 11, 2022 on Solana 💥 $115M

The attacker pumped the price of a thinly traded token, used the inflated position as collateral, and borrowed everything else in the protocol.

🎭 Oracle manipulation 🥚

Nomad Bridge

Aug 1, 2022 on Multi-chain bridge 💥 $190M

An upgrade made every message count as valid. Hundreds of copycats drained the bridge by copy-pasting the first attack.

🥚 Bad initialization 🗳️

Beanstalk

Apr 17, 2022 on Ethereum 💥 $181M

Voting power was counted at the moment of the vote. The attacker borrowed a huge amount with a flash loan, voted his own proposal through, and paid the loan back.

🗳️ Governance attack 🗝️

Ronin Bridge

Mar 23, 2022 on Ronin / Ethereum bridge 💥 $624M

Withdrawals needed 5 of 9 validator signatures. Attackers got 5 private keys and signed the withdrawals themselves. Nobody noticed for six days.

🗝️ Compromised keys 💌

Wormhole

Feb 2, 2022 on Solana / Ethereum bridge 💥 $326M

The bridge on Solana trusted a fake "signatures already verified" account, so the attacker minted 120,000 wrapped ETH backed by nothing.

💌 Signature verification 🔑

Poly Network

Aug 10, 2021 on Multi-chain bridge 💥 $611M

A cross-chain message could call a privileged function and replace the keeper that approves withdrawals. The attacker made himself keeper.

🔑 Access controlFunds returned 🥚

Parity Multisig

Nov 6, 2017 on Ethereum 💥 $150M

A shared library contract was never initialized. Someone became its owner and destroyed it, freezing every wallet that depended on it.

🥚 Bad initialization 🌀

The DAO

Jun 17, 2016 on Ethereum 💥 $60M

The first famous smart contract hack: a withdraw function sent ETH before updating the balance, so the attacker withdrew again and again.

🌀 Reentrancy
